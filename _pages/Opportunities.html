CFTC Summer Internship Program

I am actively involved in the organization of the CFTC Summer Internship Program, hosted at the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Lisbon.

The program gives BSc and MSc students in Physics, Engineering Physics, Biomedical Engineering, and related fields the opportunity to experience research first-hand by joining active research projects at CFTC. Participants work alongside researchers, gain insight into academic research, and develop practical and scientific skills in a research environment.

The program runs annually, with applications typically opening in the spring. Information about upcoming editions and available research projects can be found on the CFTC webpage.
