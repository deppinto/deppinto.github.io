I am always interested in hearing from motivated students and researchers interested in soft matter, statistical physics, biophysics and active matter. Below are some of the main routes for joining the group or developing a research project with us.

Calls, deadlines and eligibility requirements change regularly, so please consult the official programme websites for the most up-to-date information. If you are considering applying with our group, I encourage you to get in touch well before the relevant deadline.

Student Opportunities

CFTC Summer Internship Program

I am actively involved in the organization of the CFTC Summer Internship Program, hosted at the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Lisbon.

The program gives BSc and MSc students in Physics, Engineering Physics, Biomedical Engineering, and related fields the opportunity to experience research first-hand by joining active research projects at CFTC. Participants work alongside researchers, gain insight into academic research, and develop practical and scientific skills in a dynamic research environment.

The program runs annually, with applications typically opening in the spring.

CFTC webpage →

Gulbenkian New Talents Scholarships

The Gulbenkian New Talents programme supports outstanding students enrolled in higher education in Portugal and encourages their early involvement in research. Physics is one of the scientific areas covered by the programme.

Students interested in developing research related to our work are welcome to contact me to discuss possible projects and applications.

More information about New Talents →

PhD Opportunities

FCT PhD Studentships

The annual FCT PhD Studentship call is one of the main routes for funding doctoral research in Portugal. I am happy to discuss possible PhD projects with prospective candidates whose interests overlap with our research.

FCT also runs a specific funding route for PhD projects developed in a non-academic environment. This may be relevant for projects involving suitable industrial or other non-academic partners.

More information at FCT →

"la Caixa" Foundation — Doctoral INPhINIT Fellowships

The "la Caixa" Foundation Doctoral INPhINIT Fellowships support researchers wishing to pursue a PhD in Spain or Portugal. The programme includes different fellowship modalities and combines support for doctoral research with training and professional development.

Prospective candidates interested in developing a PhD project with our group are welcome to contact me to discuss possible research directions, hosting arrangements and eligibility.

More information about INPhINIT →

Postdoctoral Opportunities

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships provide an opportunity for researchers with a PhD to develop an independent research project through international mobility. I welcome enquiries from prospective applicants interested in developing an MSCA proposal hosted within the group.

Potential applicants are encouraged to get in touch early so that there is sufficient time to develop the research project and prepare a competitive proposal.

More information about MSCA Fellowships →

"la Caixa" Foundation — Junior Leader Fellowships

The "la Caixa" Foundation Postdoctoral Junior Leader Fellowships support researchers who wish to continue developing their research career in Spain or Portugal. The programme supports research together with training and professional development.

Researchers interested in developing a Junior Leader application hosted within our group are welcome to get in touch to discuss possible research projects and hosting arrangements.

More information about Junior Leader →

FCT CEEC Individual

The FCT Individual Call to Scientific Employment Stimulus (CEEC Individual) provides a route for researchers with a PhD to develop their research career at a Portuguese research institution.

Researchers interested in applying with our group are welcome to contact me to discuss potential research directions and hosting arrangements.

More information at FCT →

Other opportunities may become available throughout the year, including MSc projects, research internships, PhD projects and funded research positions.
