<p>
  Our recent work on the <strong>spontaneous rupture of cellular tissues</strong>
  has been published in <em>Nature Communications</em>. We show how collective
  cell motion can organize mechanical stresses within a tissue, eventually
  producing spontaneous ruptures without the need for an external force or an
  explicit biochemical trigger.
</p>
