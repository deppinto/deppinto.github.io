<p>
  Our group recently participated in the <strong>QSLiM Symposium 2026 —
  Quantum, Soft and Living Matter</strong> at the University of Lisbon.
  I had the opportunity to present our work on
  <strong>Collective failure in living matter</strong>, discussing some of
  our recent and ongoing research on the mechanics and fracture of cellular
  tissues.
</p>

<p>
  It was also great to see <strong>Carolina, Ricardo and
  Luís</strong> presenting their MSc research for the first time.
  Their posters explored different aspects of fracture in living matter,
  ranging from cell-based and continuum descriptions of tissue fracture to
  bioinspired synthetic materials. A great opportunity to share our work and meet others working across soft and living matter!
</p>
